What we found on the live site

  • Thin structure. Home plus one services page. No dedicated drain, leak, water heater, sewer, or emergency URLs.
  • No city pages for Mission Viejo, Irvine, Aliso Viejo, Laguna Hills, Laguna Niguel, or Rancho Santa Margarita.
  • Name / address / phone drift. Site is Lake Forest, (949) 994-9828. Some listings have pointed at Irvine, Anaheim, or an older 714 number.
  • License not on the page. Homeowners who check CSLB have to leave the site.
  • Junk link neighborhood. Hundreds of low-quality backlinks. That can hold rankings down. Treat as cleanup, not as a report card.
